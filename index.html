What we do

From raw data to information that supports decisions

Our solutions operate on three types of data. In each, the aim is the same: to find the earliest relevant change and to state where it is, how significant it is and with what confidence.

Signals

Measurements recorded over time by sensors: physical, environmental and operational quantities.

Images

Photographs, video, thermal and satellite imagery, from fixed cameras, drones or other platforms.

Documents

Technical, contractual and regulatory texts, including extensive document packages.

How we work

Five steps, from data to decision

Every solution, whatever its application, follows the same structure. The solution supports the decision; a qualified person takes it.

Acquisition

Reliable data, collected and transmitted securely.

Detection

The earliest departure from normal behaviour.

Localisation & assessment

Where, how significant, with what confidence.

Forecasting

The likely evolution, where the application allows.

Decision support

Clear, traceable evidence for those responsible.

Design objectives

What every solution is designed to provide

Objectives are expressed as measurable targets, agreed before development and verified on completion.

  • Early warning and timely intervention
  • Safety and safe use of equipment
  • Minimum downtime
  • Robustness against false alarms
  • Physical interpretability and traceability
  • Adaptability to change
  • Sustainability
  • Cost-effectiveness
